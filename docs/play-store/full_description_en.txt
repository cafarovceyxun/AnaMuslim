The Qur'an, hadith, prayer times, duas and step-by-step guides — together in one app. No ads, no analytics, and no account needed for any feature. Once content is downloaded, most of the app works offline.

The main Qur'an translation and the hadith library are in Azerbaijani; the interface is fully available in English.

<b>Qur'an reader</b>
• Full Azerbaijani translation with verse notes, plus translations in other languages
• Four layouts: verse-by-verse, mushaf (page-by-page), translation and vertical translation; book mode with page-turn animation
• Arabic scripts: Uthmani, KFQPC v1, v2, v4 and IndoPak; tajweed colouring on the Uthmani script
• Word-by-word meanings (Azerbaijani, English, Russian, Turkish) with Arabic audio for every word
• Navigate by surah, juz, hizb and page; surah info and similar verses
• Auto-scroll; turn pages with the volume keys, a keyboard or the S Pen

<b>Recitation</b>
• 16 reciters with verse tracking, repeat, playback speed and offline listening
• The Azerbaijani translation fully voiced — on its own or after the Arabic
• Background playback, a recitation player widget and Android Auto support

<b>Hadith</b>
• A library organised volume → book → chapter: Arabic text, Azerbaijani translation, source and notes
• Search across all hadith, bookmarks and reading history

<b>Prayer times and qibla</b>
• Prayer times on the home screen, on their own screen and in a widget; Hijri date
• A notification for each prayer, five adhan sounds, reminders before and after
• Save the monthly prayer calendar as an image
• Qibla direction with a compass and on a map

<b>Duas, dhikr and Asma ul-Husna</b>
• Duas selected from the hadith collections and the Qur'an, grouped by topic
• Morning and evening dhikr reminders, a counter for multi-part duas
• Asma ul-Husna — the beautiful names of Allah, with their evidence
• Search, bookmarks, notes, sharing and saving as an image

<b>Prayer and Hajj guides</b>
• Prayer guide: wudu, ghusl and tayammum with illustrations; the adhan and the steps of prayer; congregational, Friday, Eid, voluntary and funeral prayers
• Hajj and Umrah guide: steps for each day of Hajj, ihram, miqats, and tawaf and sa'y counters
• Every step comes with its hadith evidence

<b>Home, search and sharing</b>
• Verse and hadith of the day, stories and announcements; arrange the home screen your way
• Search the Qur'an, hadith and duas: filters, voice search, "find on page" in the reader
• Share a verse or hadith as text, or as an image with the built-in editor

<b>Fixing it together</b>
• Spot a mistake in a translation or hadith? Report it from the reader. Once a moderator approves it, the correction reaches everyone without an app update.
• Suggestions board: suggest new features and vote on others' suggestions

<b>Make it yours</b>
• Light, dark and system themes, seven accent palettes and Material You
• Separate sizes for Arabic and translation text; pinch to zoom
• Interface in five languages: Azerbaijani, Turkish, Russian, English and Arabic
• Backup: bookmarks, history and all settings move to a new phone

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Your data stays on your device. Location is used only for prayer times and qibla, and the calculation happens on the device itself; you can pick a city manually instead.
