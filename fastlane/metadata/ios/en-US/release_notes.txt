Azerbaijani translation audio

- The Qur'an translation is now voiced in full (all 114 chapters) and downloadable from the app.
- The player gained an audio source control: Arabic only, translation only, or both in sequence.
- The reciter sheet has a "Translation voice" section, and the download screen its own section for translations.
- Verse highlighting follows the translation as it is read.

Arabic interface

- A fifth app language; selecting it turns the whole layout right-to-left, while Azerbaijani hadith and translation text keeps its own script direction.

Hadith

- Book mode: hadith flow as continuous book text instead of cards, toggled from the index.
- Volume outline: the whole tree of books, chapters and sub-chapters, with expand-all, collapse-all and an introduction entry.

Reading and settings

- Pinch to resize text: two fingers scale the translation, three fingers the Arabic text.
- A settings sheet of the reader's own, plus a default reading mode (including "last used").
- One slider for the interface text size; the Qur'an and hadith text stay on their own settings.
- The settings screen was regrouped into "Reading", "Both readers" and "All settings".

Home and navigation

- Swipe between the tabs of the bottom bar, with transition animations.
- The verse of the day card was rebuilt: translation first, Arabic below, honouring the hadith reader's font and size.
- A new setting shows or hides that card.

Fixed

- Arabic search works again with text copied straight out of the mushaf, waqf signs and small letters included.
- The verse of the day card no longer disappears offline; the last fetched content is shown.
- Azerbaijani text is no longer mirrored in the Arabic interface.
