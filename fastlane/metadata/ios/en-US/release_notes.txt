Prayer widgets on the home screen

- Two widgets: the next prayer with a live countdown, and the whole day's times with the Hijri date.
- The timeline is handed to the system for three days ahead, so the widget stays right even if the app is not opened.

Prayer notifications

- Five sounds to pick from: the "Hadi namaza" call (said twice), a short Arabic voice alert, a melodic ringtone, a classic phone ring and a low buzz — beside the device's own sound and silence.
- Each sound can be heard before it is chosen, and one choice can be applied to every prayer at once.
- Jumu'ah is kept apart from Friday's Dhuhr, both in the list and in its notification.
- A reminder can arrive a set number of minutes before a prayer, and a follow-up a set number of minutes after it began.

Sharing a verse or a hadith

- The text colour is yours to set: a hue and a shade ruler, white and black shortcuts, or "Auto", which reads the photo's brightness and picks light or dark text.
- The preview zooms with two fingers without changing what gets saved, and the card holds portrait while the editor is open.
- Format, darkening and translation style (sans, serif, mono) are choices of their own, and the store QR code is now on by default.
- Share as text or as image from the same sheet, with a row that copies the text.

Hadith

- The current chapter's name floats at the top of the screen while you scroll, and a thin line along the bottom edge shows how much of the chapter is left.
- Narrations can be paired so each translation sits under its own Arabic, and picked one by one when sharing.

Fixes

- A hadith chapter sometimes opened at its last hadith instead of the first.
- A recitation download that stops halfway now says so, and opening the reciter again fetches the chapters that are missing.
