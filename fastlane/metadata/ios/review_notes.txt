No account is needed to use this app. Please leave "Sign-in required" unchecked.

Everything reviewable works without signing in: reading the Qur'an with the Azerbaijani
translation, the hadith library, search, bookmarks, reading history, audio recitation and all
settings. Sign-in exists for one optional feature only — submitting a correction to a verse
translation or a hadith, which a maintainer then reviews before it reaches other users.

First launch: onboarding asks for a language, a theme, and then offers to download the Azerbaijani
translation and the hadith books. Both downloads need a network connection and take under a minute
each. After that the app is fully offline — the Qur'an text, page fonts and layouts ship inside the
bundle.

Content is fetched from the project's own Supabase instance and from GitHub releases. There are no
ads, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers; telemetry was removed from the codebase
entirely.
